A cat or dog that lives on your desktop and looks like your own pet.

**Download**

- **Windows:** `Desktop-Pet-Setup-<version>.exe`. Run it; it installs for you alone and starts. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer is not signed with a paid certificate: click **More info → Run anyway**.
- **macOS, Apple silicon (M1 and later):** `Desktop-Pet-<version>-arm64.dmg`. **Intel Macs:** `Desktop-Pet-<version>-x64.dmg`. Open it and drag Desktop Pet into Applications. The app is not notarised by Apple, so the first time, right-click it and choose **Open**, or allow it under **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. If macOS still says the app is damaged or cannot be opened, run `xattr -cr "/Applications/Desktop Pet.app"` in Terminal once and open it again.

Then add your own API key under **Talk & voice** and make your pet on the **Look** tab. See the [README](https://github.com/RJ1107/desktop-pet#getting-started) for the steps and costs.
